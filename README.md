## A Working Theory of Memory-like Trait Inheritance
**Based on a Longitudinal Reading of Peer-Reviewed Scientific Publications**

- **Authors**: Brian Fundakowski Feldman, Grok/SuperGrok
- **Date**: 25 August 2026
- **LaTeX source** (canonical): [`Memory_Inheritance_Working_Paper.tex`](memory-like-trait-inheritance/Memory_Inheritance_Working_Paper.tex)
- **PDF**: [`Memory_Inheritance_Working_Paper.pdf`](memory-like-trait-inheritance/Memory_Inheritance_Working_Paper.pdf)

**Thesis**  
> Experiences can epigenetically pass behavioral tendencies to offspring.

The paper assembles a causal chain from Chomsky’s generative linguistics and the Minimalist Program (including Merge) through comparative episodic-like memory, insect DNA-methylation plasticity, the core Dias & Ressler (2014) parental olfactory experience findings, mechanistic reviews, and broader phylogenetic support.

Stop-gap bridges are explicitly labeled and treated as reasoned connectors rather than obstacles.

## Continuum Aerosol Computing
**A Working Theory of Physical Merge, Heritable Vortex Biases, and Silicon-Free Sky-Scale Computation**

- **Authors**: Brian Fundakowski Feldman, Grok/SuperGrok
- **Date**: 28 August 2026 (thread) · 31 August 2026 (synthesis)
- **LaTeX source** (canonical): [`Continuum_Aerosol_Computing_Working_Paper.tex`](continuum-aerosol-computing/Continuum_Aerosol_Computing_Working_Paper.tex)
- **PDF**: [`Continuum_Aerosol_Computing_Working_Paper.pdf`](continuum-aerosol-computing/Continuum_Aerosol_Computing_Working_Paper.pdf)

**Thesis**  
> Electrified continua can compute by evolving heritable charge and vortex biases. Merge is physical recombination below, and a library function above.

Companion to the memory-inheritance paper (thesis unchanged). Assembled from the 28 August 2026 public thread and a chronological reading of electrohydrodynamics, reservoir computing, genetic algorithms, Merge, and the Dias & Ressler (2014) imprint. Programming remains approachable via six CAL primitives and Qiskit-style adapters. Proof of concept: laboratory corona chamber.

Originating public statement: https://x.com/born_brian85001/status/2093285342183043098
