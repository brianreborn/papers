## Key Design Decisions

- **Stop-gaps are bridges, not blockers.** Three explicit stop-gaps (Interface, Cross-species homology, Content vs Tendency) are labeled and described but deliberately framed as reasoned connectors that close empirical gaps.
- **DNA methylation** is treated as a primary candidate carrier throughout the literature assembly and discussion.
- **LaTeX over pure PDF generation.** The `.tex` file is the source of truth; the PDF is a derived artifact. This choice was made so the paper remains editable, version-controllable, and universally archival.
- **Skill for reproducibility.** The full process (thesis, ordered literature with DOIs, stop-gap wording, LaTeX rules, GitHub publication steps, and archival fallbacks via archive.org / Google Scholar) is encoded in a skill so the paper can be faithfully regenerated later.
- **Authorship transparency.** Primary authorship rests with Brian (originator of the claim and director of the inquiry). Grok/SuperGrok is listed as a single co-author entry reflecting substantial synthesis, structuring, writing, diagramming, and process formalization.
