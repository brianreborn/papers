# Core Literature (ordered)

Use this list as the default backbone. Prefer DOIs. Fall back to archive.org / Google Scholar when a live link fails.

1. Chomsky, N. (1957). *Syntactic Structures*. Mouton.  
   https://archive.org/details/syntacticstructu0000chom  
   Role: innate biological structure of language/mind

2. Chomsky, N. (1965). *Aspects of the Theory of Syntax*. MIT Press.  
   https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262527408/aspects-of-the-theory-of-syntax/  
   Role: competence–performance, deep structure

3. Chomsky, N. (1968). *Language and Mind*. Harcourt.  
   https://archive.org/details/languagemind00chom  
   Role: language as biological organ of mind

4. Clayton, N. S., & Dickinson, A. (1998). Episodic-like memory during cache recovery by scrub jays. *Nature* 395:272–274.  
   https://doi.org/10.1038/26216  
   Role: structured experiential traces in non-human animals

5. Herb, B. R., et al. (2012). Reversible switching between epigenetic states in honeybee behavioral subcastes. *Nat. Neurosci.* 15:1371–1373.  
   https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.3218  
   Role: reversible DNA-methylation plasticity tied to behavior

6. Related honeybee work (2012). DNA methylation mediates discriminatory power of associative long-term memory. *PLoS ONE*.  
   https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0039349  
   Role: methylation shapes odor-specificity of memory

7. Dias, B. G., & Ressler, K. J. (2014). Parental olfactory experience influences behavior and neural structure in subsequent generations. *Nat. Neurosci.* 17:89–96.  
   https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.3594  
   Role: core empirical node — parental experience → germline CpG hypomethylation → offspring phenotype

8. Dias, B. G., et al. (2015). Epigenetic mechanisms underlying learning and the inheritance of learned behaviors. *Trends Neurosci.* 38:96–107.  
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tins.2014.12.003  
   Role: molecular pathways generalizing the 2014 finding

9. Berwick, R. C., & Chomsky, N. (2016). *Why Only Us*. MIT Press.  
   https://doi.org/10.7551/mitpress/9780262034241.001.0001  
   Role: evolutionary framing of innate computational core

10. Fitz-James, M. H., & Cavalli, G. (2022). Molecular mechanisms of transgenerational epigenetic inheritance. *Nat. Rev. Genet.* 23:325–341.  
    https://doi.org/10.1038/s41576-021-00438-5  
    Role: scaffolding for germline survival of marks (methylation, histones, ncRNA)

11. Insect / olfactory updates (Feng et al. & related, 2022–23). *Insects* and concurrent literature.  
    https://www.mdpi.com/journal/insects  
    Role: keep comparative insect side current

12. Yang-related / eLife work (2023). Opposing epigenetic forces on OR choice; fear-conditioning bias of OSN fate.  
    https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.87445  
    Role: strengthens olfactory-receptor pathway

13. Deshe, N., et al. (2023). Inheritance of associative memories and acquired cellular changes in *C. elegans*. *Nat. Commun.* 14:4232.  
    https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-39804-8  
    Role: phylogenetic broadening; histone methylation involvement
