# Core Literature (ordered) — Continuum Aerosol Computing

Prefer DOIs. Fall back to archive.org / publisher pages when a live link fails.

1. Maxwell, J. C. (1873). *A Treatise on Electricity and Magnetism*. Clarendon.
   https://archive.org/details/electricandmagne01maxwrich
   Role: EM half of the coupled substrate; vortices as physical structure

2. Batchelor, G. K. (1967). *An Introduction to Fluid Dynamics*. CUP.
   https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511800955
   Role: fluid half; turbulence as parallelism

3. Holland, J. H. (1975). *Adaptation in Natural and Artificial Systems*.
   https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262581110/adaptation-in-natural-and-artificial-systems/
   Role: discrete GAs as higher-level library

4. Nix, A. E., & Vose, M. D. (1992). Modeling genetic algorithms with Markov chains. *Ann. Math. Artif. Intell.* 5:79–88.
   https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01530781
   Role: generational transitions as Markov chains

5. Chomsky, N. (1995). *The Minimalist Program*. MIT Press.
   https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262527347/the-minimalist-program/
   Role: names Merge; relocated here as physical recombination in a different domain

6. Saville, D. A. (1997). Electrohydrodynamics: The Taylor–Melcher leaky dielectric model. *Annu. Rev. Fluid Mech.* 29:27–64.
   https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.fluid.29.1.27
   Role: continuum EHD of electrified droplets

7. Jaeger, H. (2001). The “echo state” approach to analysing and training recurrent neural networks. *GMD Report* 148.
   https://www.ai.rug.nl/minds/uploads/EchoStatesTechRep.pdf
   Role: reservoir analogue of set-initials / evolve / measure

8. Maass, W., Natschläger, T., & Markram, H. (2002). Real-time computing without stable states. *Neural Computation* 14:2531–2560.
   https://doi.org/10.1162/089976602760407955
   Role: liquid-state machine as model for a turbulent fluid computer

9. Dias, B. G., & Ressler, K. J. (2014). Parental olfactory experience influences behavior and neural structure in subsequent generations. *Nat. Neurosci.* 17:89–96.
   https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.3594
   Role: biological template for inject(experience)

10. Qiskit contributors. Qiskit SDK.
    https://github.com/Qiskit/qiskit
    Role: adapter pattern (circuit → backend)

11. Guan, Y., Vaddi, R. S., Aliseda, A., & Novosselov, I. (2018). Experimental and numerical investigation of electrohydrodynamic flow in a point-to-ring corona discharge. *Phys. Rev. Fluids* 3:043701.
    https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevFluids.3.043701
    Role: lab corona chamber as existing EHD aggregate

12. Feldman, B. F., & Grok/SuperGrok (2026). *A Working Theory of Memory-like Trait Inheritance*.
    https://github.com/brianreborn/papers
    Role: companion thesis (unchanged)
